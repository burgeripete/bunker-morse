How to share DayZ's sound
  1. Press Start in Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  2. Pick the DayZ window if your browser offers to share its audio. Otherwise pick Entire screen.
  3. Turn on the share audio option in the share dialog. Without it, the page hears nothing.
  4. Keep this page open while you play. A background tab is fine.

Everything runs in this page. Nothing you share is uploaded or stored, and the page never plays sound.
