How to use
  1. Get a radio Find a Handheld Transceiver or a Field Transceiver, and make sure it has power.
  2. Tune to 80 MHz at night Turn the radio on and set it to 80 MHz. The broadcast is only on at night, and sounds like slow beeps.
  3. Press Start Use the button above, in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Keep this page open while you play.
  4. Share DayZ's sound Pick the DayZ window if the dialog offers its audio, otherwise pick Entire screen. Turn on the share audio option.
  5. Keep listening Stay on the frequency. Each bunker takes about 80 seconds, and all three repeat about every 4 minutes.
  6. Find the bunker Click a card's coordinates to open them on the map, then use its keypad code. Digits turn green once a repeat confirms them.

Everything runs in this page. Nothing you share is uploaded or stored, and the page never plays sound.
